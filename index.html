About Us

A home for slow, delicious days.

Aurelia began as a small neighbourhood café with a single espresso machine and a big idea: that hospitality is a craft. Today it is a restaurant, a roastery and a private resort — still led by the same kitchen, the same people and the same obsession with detail.

We cook with what the land and sea offer each morning, roast our beans in small batches and design every room so that the best part of your stay is simply being there.
